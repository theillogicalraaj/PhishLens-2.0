# PhishLens Roadmap

- Safe simulation campaign management
- Synthetic recipients
- Behavioral event tracking
- Dashboard analytics
- Explainable risk scoring
- Synthetic ML baseline
- PDF reporting

1. Calibrated model trained on approved organizational data.
2. Department and cohort benchmarking.
3. Time-to-report analytics.
4. Repeat-behavior detection.
5. Content-risk tagging without generating credential-theft payloads.
6. SSO/RBAC and enterprise audit trails.
7. Background jobs and message queues.
8. SIEM integration for awareness events.
9. Privacy-preserving analytics and retention controls.
10. A/B testing of approved training interventions.
